▣ FIELD MEASURE 3D

6DoF POSE · RAY CAST · NOT A 2D PHOTO

Live camera. Real millimetres in 3D.

A printed marker is the world origin. The phone solves its position and rotation every frame. Taps become rays in 3D: they hit the floor plane, or climb a vertical for height.

  1. Download the marker. Print at 100% / actual size.
  2. Lay it flat on the table (ruler test) or floor (room).
  3. Point the rear camera at it until the XYZ axes lock.
  4. Walk / orbit — keep the marker in view — then tap in 3D.
